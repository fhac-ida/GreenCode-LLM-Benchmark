# LLM Energy Benchmark – Prompt Set
# Lines starting with # are ignored.
# Deliberately diverse: short/factual, medium/reasoning, long/generative.

# ── Tier 1: Short factual ────────────────────────────────────────────────────
What is the capital of France?
What year was the C programming language created?
Name three planets in our solar system.
What is 17 multiplied by 23?
Who wrote the novel "1984"?

# ── Tier 2: Medium reasoning ─────────────────────────────────────────────────
Explain the difference between TCP and UDP in two sentences.
What are the main advantages of using containers over virtual machines?
Describe how HTTPS protects data in transit.
What is gradient descent and why is it used in machine learning?
Summarise the causes of World War I in three bullet points.

# ── Tier 3: Long generation ───────────────────────────────────────────────────
Write a Python function that checks whether a given string is a palindrome. Include docstring and tests.
Explain how a transformer neural network works. Cover attention mechanisms, tokenisation, and training.
Write a short story (about 200 words) about a robot who discovers music for the first time.
List and briefly explain ten common software design patterns with a one-line example each.
Describe the pros and cons of renewable energy sources: solar, wind, hydroelectric, and geothermal.

# ── Tier 4: Reasoning-intensive (scored, see reasoning_prompts.csv) ──────────
A train travels 60 km in the first hour and 90 km in the second hour. What is its average speed in km/h over the two hours? Give only the final number.
A shop had 120 apples. It sold 45% of them in the morning and 30 more in the afternoon. How many apples are left? Give only the final number.
Sarah has 3 times as many marbles as Tom. Together they have 48 marbles. How many marbles does Tom have? Give only the final number.
A rectangular garden is 12 meters long and 8 meters wide. A path 1 meter wide runs around the inside edge of the garden. What is the area in square meters of the garden excluding the path? Give only the final number.
If a recipe needs 250 grams of flour for 5 servings, how many grams are needed for 12 servings? Give only the final number.
A tank is filled by pipe A in 6 hours and by pipe B in 3 hours. If both pipes run together, how many hours does it take to fill the tank? Give the answer rounded to one decimal place.
There are 15 people at a party. Each person shakes hands exactly once with every other person. How many handshakes occur in total? Give only the final number.
A car depreciates by 20% of its value each year. If it is worth 20000 now, what will it be worth after 2 years, rounded to the nearest whole number? Give only the final number.
Two numbers have a sum of 42 and a difference of 8. What is the larger number? Give only the final number.
A store increases the price of an item by 25% and then offers a 20% discount on the new price. If the original price was 80, what is the final price? Give only the final number.
A cyclist rides at 18 km/h for 40 minutes. How many kilometers does she travel? Give only the final number.
In a class of 30 students, 18 study French and 12 study Spanish, with 5 studying both. How many students study neither language? Give only the final number.
A ladder 10 meters long leans against a wall with its foot 6 meters from the wall's base. How many meters up the wall does the ladder reach? Give only the final number.
A jar contains red and blue marbles in a ratio of 3:5. If there are 24 red marbles, how many blue marbles are there? Give only the final number.
A worker is paid 15 per hour for the first 8 hours and 22.5 per hour for each additional hour. How much is earned for a 10-hour day? Give only the final number.
